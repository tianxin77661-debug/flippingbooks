# What Is a Flipbook?

A **flipbook** is a digital reading experience that presents document pages with navigation and, depending on the platform, a page flip effect. This guide explains what a flipbook is, how it differs from a PDF, and where an online flipbook can be useful.
![online-flipbook-software-vs-pdf-overview.png](https://files.seeusercontent.com/2026/08/20/M3tn/online-flipbook-software-vs-pdf-.png)
## What Is a Flipbook?

A flipbook is an interactive digital publication designed to reproduce some of the visual experience of turning pages in a printed document.

A PDF is primarily a document format. A flipbook adds a presentation layer around that content, such as page navigation, responsive reading, and a page flip effect.

Depending on the publishing platform, a digital flipbook can also include links, images, media, navigation controls, and other interactive elements.

## Flip Book vs. Flipbook

You may see both **flip book** and **flipbook** online.

- **Flipbook** is the common form used for digital publishing software and online reading experiences.
- **Flip book** can also describe a traditional paper animation made from sequential drawings.

The context matters. A paper flip book and an online flipbook share the idea of sequential pages, but they are different formats.

## Flipbook vs. PDF

| PDF | Digital flipbook |
| --- | --- |
| Document file format | Interactive reading experience |
| Standard document navigation | Page-oriented visual navigation |
| Useful for archiving and sharing files | Useful for online presentation |
| Usually feels like a document | Can feel more like a digital publication |

A useful workflow is to prepare content as a PDF and then publish it as an online flipbook when a more engaging reading experience is needed.

## What Is an Online Flipbook?

An **online flipbook** is a flipbook that readers access through a web browser.

It can be useful for digital magazines, brochures, catalogs, portfolios, educational materials, reports, and other visual documents.

## What Makes a Digital Flipbook Interactive?

The exact features depend on the flipbook software or publishing platform, but a digital flipbook can combine:

- Page navigation
- Page flip effects
- Responsive layouts
- Images and illustrations
- Clickable links
- Interactive elements
- Shareable URLs
- Online presentation of PDF-based content

## Flipbook Maker and Flipbook Software

A **flipbook maker** is a tool that converts or publishes content into an interactive flipbook.

A typical workflow is:

1. Prepare a document, PDF, or image-based publication.
2. Upload the source content.
3. Generate the digital flipbook.
4. Review the reading experience.
5. Publish and share the result.

## FAQ

### Is a flipbook a PDF?

Not exactly. A PDF is a document format, while a flipbook is a digital presentation and reading experience that can be created from a PDF.

### What is a page flip effect?

A page flip effect is a visual transition that simulates turning from one page to another.

### Can a flipbook be viewed online?

Yes. An online flipbook can be published to a web-based reader and shared through a URL when the publishing platform provides public sharing.
