## What Is a Flipbook?

A **flipbook** is a digital publication that presents pages in a book-like reading experience, often with page-turn animation and interactive navigation.

A PDF is primarily a document format. A flipbook focuses on the **presentation and reading experience** of that document.

That difference makes a digital flipbook useful when you want readers to browse content visually, move through pages naturally, and access a publication from a browser or mobile device.

## How Do You Make a Flipbook?

There are two common ways to think about making a flipbook:

### 1. Physical flipbooks

A traditional flipbook is made from a sequence of drawings or images. When the pages are moved quickly, the images create the illusion of motion.

### 2. Digital flipbooks

A digital flipbook recreates the page-by-page reading experience in a browser. Instead of manually flipping paper pages, readers can navigate through an interactive publication on desktop or mobile devices.

For most modern publishing workflows, a **PDF to flipbook** process is a practical way to reuse existing documents without rebuilding every page from scratch.
